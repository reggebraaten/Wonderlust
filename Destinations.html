Places I Love to Visit

  • Hawaii
  • Spain
  • Mexico
  • France
  • United States

Hawaii is one of my favorite places to visit because I love the ocean, warm weather and surfing.I lived in Hawaii off and on when I was in my twenties. It changed my life and I learned so much about myself living there. I have also traveled to France and Spain which was amazing. We visited palaces and the oldest gothic cathedral in the world that had the crown jewels inside. I have driven across the United States and been all over Mexico as well. Some of my favorite places in the United States are Bend, OR, Sedona, AZ, Baker Lake, WA, the Smokey mountains in West Virginia and the northern California Coast. I also love Puerto Vallarta and Sayulita, in Mexico.
