My Wanderlust

I am a mom of three beautiful children living in rural Montana, and I have always loved exploring new places and meeting new people. I believe there is something exciting about discovering what is waiting around the next corner and making memories along the way.

Things That Make Me Happy

Some of my favorite things are swimming, surfing, listening to music, hiking and reading. Most of all, I love spending time with my kids and sharing new experiences with them.

Sharing the Spirit of Wanderlust

One of my greatest joys is teaching my kids the spirit of wanderlust. I want them to be curious about the world, excited to explore new places and always ready for their next adventure!

A Few of My Favorite Things

  • Exploring new places
  • Swimming and surfing
  • Hiking and spending time outdoors
  • Music and reading
  • Making memories with my kids
